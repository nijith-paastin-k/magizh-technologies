FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before starting a project with Magizh Technologies.

How long does it take to develop an Android app?

Development timelines vary based on project complexity. A simple app typically takes 4-8 weeks, while complex applications with multiple features can take 3-6 months. We provide a detailed timeline during discovery.

How much do your services cost?

Every project is unique, so we provide custom quotes based on your requirements. We work with budgets ranging from ₹50,000 to ₹5,00,000+ depending on scope and complexity.

Do you provide maintenance after launch?

Yes! We offer ongoing maintenance and support packages including bug fixes, security patches, performance updates, and feature enhancements.

How do we communicate during the project?

We use weekly updates, video calls for milestone reviews, WhatsApp/Email for daily communication, and project management tools for task tracking.

Do you sign NDAs?

Absolutely. We're happy to sign Non-Disclosure Agreements before starting any project.

What if I need changes after delivery?

We offer post-launch support and can accommodate enhancements, design changes, or bug fixes as part of our maintenance packages.

Who owns the code after completion?

You own 100% of the source code, design assets, and intellectual property. We deliver complete documentation and assets.

What technologies do you specialize in?

Android: Kotlin, Java, Jetpack Compose, MVVM. Web: Python, Django, Flask. APIs: REST, WebSocket, OAuth 2.0. Databases: PostgreSQL, SQLite, Room, Firebase. DevOps: CI/CD, Git, Docker, AWS.

Can you work on existing apps?

Yes! We can take over existing Android apps or web applications for maintenance, feature additions, performance optimization, or complete rebuilds.

How do I get started?

Contact us for a free consultation. We'll understand your requirements and provide a project quote.
